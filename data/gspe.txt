COMPANY OVERVIEW – GSPE (PT Graha Sumber Prima Elektronik)

Established: 1996
Facilities: 20,000m² factory in Tangerang Selatan
Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, ISO 45001:2018, TKDN (PDU, Containment, LVMDP, MCC)
Core Competency: Power systems, electrical panels, data center infrastructure, and enclosures

SERVICES OFFERED
- Consultation & Design (Data center, panel, rack)
- Engineering & Fabrication (Metal works, assembly, wiring)
- Installation & Commissioning (On-site installation and testing)
- Project Management (ISO-compliant project handling)
- Repair Center (Upgrades, rectifier and panel repair)
- 24/7 Customer Response Center (Monitoring rectifier & battery)
- Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) (Complete pre-delivery testing)
- Logistics & Quality Assurance (Delivery, inspection, compliance)

7. TDK-LAMBDA POWER PRODUCTS (Authorized Distributor)
- AC-DC Power Supplies: Available in unit types, PC board types, power module types, on-board types, and DIN rail mount types, ranging from several watts to over 2 kW.
- DC-DC Converters: High-reliability converters with isolated and non-isolated types, suitable for various applications.
- High Voltage Power Supplies: Programmable capacitor charging and AC-DC power supplies, including the ALE series.
- Programmable Electronic Loads: SFL series featuring fast response and linear current change from zero volts, with load power ratings of 300 W / 1 kW.
- Bidirectional Converters: EZA series capable of both step-down and step-up operations in a single unit.
- Power Line EMC Filters: Designed to suppress electromagnetic interference in power lines.
- Accessories: Includes expansion modules, evaluation boards, heatsinks, cables and harnesses, power supply mounting plates, and compatible brackets.
